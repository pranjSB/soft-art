# About the Author

I've spent several years in software engineering along with test automation and quality engineering. 
I noticed that most automation frameworks focus almost entirely on **running tests**. 
However, much less attention is given to understanding **why tests fail**, **how stability evolves over time**, 
and **how teams can trust their CI pipelines**.

I've been building **SentinelCore** and **ContractGuard** as my hobby projects.

SentinelCore was created as part of my exploration into **test reliability engineering**. SentinelCore is my attempt to think about automation from a different perspective — not just execution, but **intelligence around execution**.

In parallel, ContractGuard is a backend system focused on runtime API contract validation and drift detection. While SentinelCore looks at reliability from a testing and CI perspective, ContractGuard approaches it at the service layer by ensuring APIs adhere to defined contracts over time.

Together, these explorations focus on improving system reliability from both execution and runtime perspectives.

The project explores ideas such as:

- identifying flaky tests through historical signals
- classifying failures beyond simple pass/fail
- improving CI pipeline observability
- reducing debugging time for engineering teams

This repository is not intended to be a full production platform. Instead, it serves as a 
**technical exploration of how automation systems can evolve into reliability platforms**.

## Why SentinelCore and ContractGuard

Automation frameworks are widely used, but the next step for many engineering teams is understanding the behavior of those frameworks over time. SentinelCore explores that direction, while ContractGuard extends the same philosophy to backend systems by focusing on contract enforcement and runtime reliability.
